Magic Sphere

Privacy Policy | Effective Date: October 16, 2026

Welcome to Magic Sphere ("we", "our", or "the application"), an entertainment application designed to simulate fortune-telling and answer-revealing experiences inspired by the classic Magic 8-Ball.

Your privacy is our utmost priority. This Privacy Policy details how we handle permissions, device sensors, and information when you use our mobile application.

1. Permissions & On-Device Processing

Microphone Permission (android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO):
Magic Sphere offers an optional voice query feature that allows you to speak your question aloud before asking the mystical sphere.
  • Audio speech recognition is processed entirely on your device via the standard Android SpeechRecognizer framework.
  • We DO NOT record, store, collect, or transmit your voice recordings or audio files to any server.
  • Voice data is discarded immediately once recognized text is obtained. Granting microphone permission is completely optional; you can also use the app silently at any time without speech.

2. Device Sensors & Accelerometer

The application accesses your device's built-in Accelerometer and motion sensors strictly in real time to detect when you physically shake your phone.

  • Sensor measurements are calculated in volatile memory solely for shake threshold detection.
  • No accelerometer or motion data is ever logged, stored, or shared.

3. Local Storage & Prediction History

The application saves your recent prediction history and settings (such as sound effects toggle and vibration preferences) strictly locally on your device using Android's private SharedPreferences storage.

  • This data never leaves your device.
  • You can erase this history at any time by clearing the application's storage in your device settings or by uninstalling the application.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Magic Sphere uses Google AdMob to display occasional interstitial advertisements. Google AdMob may collect and process pseudonymous identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID - AAID) and device diagnostic information to deliver relevant ads and detect fraud, in compliance with Google Play Developer policies and applicable privacy laws.

For further details regarding how Google processes advertising data, please review:
Google Advertising & Privacy Policy

5. In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing)

The application offers an optional one-time purchase to permanently remove all advertisements ("Ad-Free Version").

  • All financial transactions are handled exclusively and securely by the Google Play In-App Billing system.
  • The developer never receives, accesses, or stores your credit card, billing address, or payment credentials.

6. Children's Privacy

Magic Sphere is intended as a general entertainment application and does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe your child has provided personal information to us, please contact us immediately, and we will promptly take appropriate action.

7. GDPR & CCPA Rights

Under regulations such as the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), users have rights to know what data is collected and request its deletion. Because Magic Sphere does not maintain private user databases or collect personal identifiable information (PII), we do not possess user profiles to disclose or delete. You retain full control over your device identifier through your Android OS privacy settings.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
