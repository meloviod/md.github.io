# Privacy Policy for Larry Escape

* **Application:** Larry Escape
* **Package Name:** `com.larry.escape`
* **Effective Date:** October 13, 2026
* **Last Updated:** October 13, 2026

---

## 1. Introduction
Welcome to **Larry Escape** ("the Game", "we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any information that may be collected while you enjoy our puzzle game.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you download, install, and play **Larry Escape** on Android devices.

> [!NOTE]
> **Summary in Plain English:** We do not collect, sell, or store your personal information (such as your name, email, or physical address) on our servers. Your game progress and custom puzzles stay strictly on your device. Third-party advertising (Google AdMob) and payment processing (Google Play) are handled strictly by trusted Google services according to their privacy policies.

---

## 2. Information We Do NOT Collect
The Game does not require user registration or account creation. We do not collect or store:
* Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as your real name, physical address, phone number, or email address.
* Biometric data, contacts, media library, photos, or audio recordings.
* Precise GPS location data.

---

## 3. Information Stored Locally on Your Device
To provide smooth gameplay and remember your achievements, the Game saves data locally in your device's private storage (Android `SharedPreferences`):
* Completed levels, campaign progress, and earned star ratings.
* Personal move records (optimal par scores).
* Custom levels created by you in the built-in Level Editor.
* Available hint balance and unlocked features.
* User preferences: selected language, theme, sound/haptic toggles, and virtual D-pad controls.

This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to our servers. You can reset all local progress at any time directly in the game's **Settings > Reset All Data** menu.

---

## 4. Third-Party Services & Advertising
The Game utilizes trusted third-party SDKs provided by Google LLC to provide advertising and in-app purchase capabilities:

### A. Google AdMob (Advertising)
We use **Google AdMob** to display non-intrusive advertisements (banners, interstitials, and rewarded videos for hints). AdMob may collect and process information such as:
* Android Advertising ID (AAID).
* IP address (used to infer general coarse location, e.g., country or city).
* Device information (model, operating system version, screen resolution, mobile carrier).
* Ad interaction metrics (views, clicks, diagnostic crash logs).

This data is used by Google to serve ads, prevent fraud, and measure ad performance. You can review Google's privacy practices here:
* [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
* [How Google Uses Information From Apps](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads)

### B. Google Play In-App Billing (Shop Purchases)
When you purchase in-game items (such as ad removal or premium themes), all financial transactions are processed directly by **Google Play**. We do not collect, view, or store your credit card numbers, bank details, or billing addresses.

---

## 5. Device Permissions Used
The Game requests only the minimal permissions required for operation:
* `android.permission.INTERNET`: Required to load advertisements from Google AdMob and verify purchase receipts with Google Play.
* `android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`: Required to detect whether an internet connection is active before requesting ads.
* `android.permission.VIBRATE`: Used solely to provide tactile haptic feedback during moves and interactions. This can be disabled at any time in the Game Settings.

---

## 6. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)
**Larry Escape** is designed as a family-friendly puzzle game suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union).

If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided personal information to third-party services through the game, please contact us, and we will take immediate steps to address your concern.

---

## 7. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA / CPRA)
Depending on your jurisdiction (such as the European Economic Area, the UK, or California, USA), you have rights regarding your data:
* **Opting Out of Personalized Advertising:** You can opt out of interest-based ads or reset your Google Advertising ID on your Android device at any time by navigating to: `Settings > Google > Ads > Delete advertising ID` (or `Reset advertising ID`).
* **Right to Deletion:** You can delete all locally stored game data at any time via **Settings > Reset All Data** in the game, or by clearing app data in Android Application Settings.
* **Ad-Free Experience:** You can completely disable all advertising inside the game by purchasing the ad removal options in the Game Shop.
