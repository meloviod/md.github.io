1. Overview & Commitment

This Privacy Policy explains how Alchemist of Color ("the Game", "we", "our", or "us", package ID com.alchemistofcolor) handles information when you play our game on Android mobile devices.

We respect your privacy. We do not collect, harvest, sell, or rent any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, personal email address, physical address, phone number, contacts, precise GPS location, photos, or biometric data.

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

To provide a persistent, seamless puzzle gaming experience that functions completely offline without requiring an account or internet login, the Game stores non-personal game progress locally on your device:

  • Game Progression: High scores for each game mode (4×4, 5×5, 6×6, Quests, Blitz), current tile crucible layout, move counts, and active quest completion progress.
  • Inventory & Palette: Discovered color pigments and cosmic elements (Tiers 1–12), synthesis statistics, in-game Gold Dust currency, and booster counts (Rewinds, Hammers, Pipettes, Rainbows).
  • Game Settings: Sound and music toggles, selected user interface language, and ad-free entitlement status.

This data is stored solely inside your device's private, sandbox application storage (via Android SharedPreferences). We do not transmit your game board state to external developer servers.

3. Third-Party Services & Monetization

The Game integrates industry-standard Google Play development libraries to support ad-funded free play and optional digital purchases:

AdMob Google Mobile Ads (AdMob):

The Game uses Google AdMob to display banner advertisements, interstitial transitions, and optional rewarded videos (e.g., "Second Wind" revives or bonus gold). Google AdMob may collect and process device technical data, such as:

  • Device advertising identifiers (Google Advertising ID / GAID).
  • Approximate geographical location inferred from IP address (at country/city level).
  • App diagnostic information, crash logs, and ad interaction metrics to combat fraud and deliver relevant ads.

You can review Google's privacy practices at: Google Privacy & Terms and How Google uses information from sites or apps.

Billing Google Play In-App Billing:

If you purchase optional digital goods (such as removing banner ads forever for $0.49 or purchasing alchemy starter bundles), the entire payment transaction is handled directly and securely by Google Play Billing Library 8.0+. We do not have access to, nor do we collect or store, your credit card numbers, billing addresses, or banking details.

4. Currency and SIM Information

The Game references the country code associated with your device's cellular network (via Android TelephonyManager.simCountryIso) strictly on-device to format localized pricing indicators (such as showing prices in local currency like $, €, £, ₴, ₽, etc.) even when the device language differs from your location. This country ISO code is processed transiently on-device and is never transmitted or sold.

5. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & Family Policy)

Alchemist of Color is a family-friendly color logic and puzzle game suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from children under the age of 13. The advertising served by Google AdMob complies with applicable Google Play Families and COPPA restrictions.

6. Data Retention, Control & Deletion Rights

Because all gameplay state is stored on your own device, you have complete and immediate control over your data:

  • Resetting or Deleting Data: You can completely and irreversibly erase all stored game data at any time by going to your Android device Settings → Apps → Alchemist of Color → Storage → Clear Data, or by uninstalling the Game.
  • Advertising Privacy: You can reset or delete your Google Advertising ID at any time via Android Settings → Google → Ads.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect product enhancements, legal updates, or changes in store policies. Any updates will be published directly on this page with an updated "Effective Date".
