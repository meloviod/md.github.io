Privacy Policy

Game: World Treasures («Сокровища миров») | Last updated: October 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application World Treasures («Сокровища миров») ("we", "our", or "the Game") handles your information when you download, install, and play our game. We are strongly committed to protecting the privacy of our players.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect personal identifying information such as:

  • Your real name, postal address, or phone number
  • Your email address (unless you voluntarily contact our customer support)
  • Your contacts, microphone audio, camera photos, or precise GPS location

2. Technical Data & On-Device Operations

To ensure smooth gameplay, correct level progress saving, and accurate currency formatting:

  • Game Progress & Preferences: Your level completions, stars, unlocked worlds, and booster inventory are saved securely in your local on-device database (Room / DataStore) and never sent to unauthorized third-party servers.
  • Country Code for Local Currency Display: The Game queries the device carrier/SIM card country ISO code (e.g., UA for Ukraine, PL for Poland, US for USA) solely on your device to display shop package prices in your local currency format (such as ₴, zł, €, $). This check is performed strictly on your local device.

3. Third-Party Services and Advertising

To provide free content, optional rewarded video ads, and secure in-app purchases, our game integrates trusted, industry-standard third-party SDKs provided by Google:

  • Google AdMob (Mobile Ads): Used to display banner advertisements, interstitial ads, and rewarded video ads. AdMob may collect and process device advertising identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID / GAID), IP address, and basic device metrics to deliver relevant, safe advertisements. For more details, please visit Google Privacy & Terms and AdMob Policies.
  • Google Play In-App Billing: Used to process purchases of virtual currency and booster packs securely through your Google Play Account. All financial transactions and credit card details are handled exclusively by Google Play Services; the Game never sees, receives, or stores your banking or credit card information.

Opting Out of Personalized Advertising: You can reset your Google Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads at any time via your device settings: Settings → Google → Ads → Reset Advertising ID or Delete Advertising ID.

4. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K)

Our game is designed as a family-friendly match-3 puzzle adventure suitable for players of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us, and we will promptly take steps to delete any such information.

5. European Union Users (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA) or the United Kingdom, you have certain rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), including:

  • The right to know what personal data is processed by ad networks
  • The right to request deletion of data held by third-party processors
  • The right to withdraw consent for targeted ads at any time via device settings

6. California Residents (CCPA / CPRA)

We do not sell your personal information. Third-party ad partners (such as Google AdMob) may collect unique device identifiers for advertising purposes under California law. You may exercise your "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" preference through your Android device settings.

7. Data Security

We take all reasonable precautions to safeguard your game progress and ensure our software is secure, free from malware, and compliant with all Google Play Developer Program Policies.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in legal requirements or game features. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
