1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application Smart Resume Assistant & CV Builder (Package ID: com.dmitry.myresume), developed by Dmitry. This document informs you regarding our policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of Personal Information for anyone using our Application.

By downloading, installing, or using the Application, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information Collection and Use

A. Information You Provide in Resumes

When creating or editing a resume, you may input personal data including your full name, email address, phone number, city, work history, education, skills, and languages.

Important: All information you enter is stored exclusively in private local storage on your device using Android's native SharedPreferences and SQLite/internal files. We do not upload, sync, or transmit your resume contents to any external servers or third-party cloud services.

B. Regional Format Detection (No GPS Location)

To provide convenient telephone masks, salary currency symbols (e.g. ₴, $, £, €, ₸), and popular local university presets, the application queries the device's ISO country code through standard Android system APIs (TelephonyManager SIM/network country code and Locale.getDefault().getCountry()).

The Application does not request or access fine or coarse GPS coordinates. You can freely change your active region at any time via the in-app country selector.

C. Third-Party Services

The Application uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you:

Partner / Service Purpose Data Collected Privacy Policy
Google AdMob Displaying interstitial and banner advertisements Advertising ID (AAID), diagnostic metrics, coarse IP-derived region Google Ads Privacy
Google Play In-App Billing Processing the "Remove Ads" one-time purchase ($1.00 USD) Purchase tokens, transaction status (handled securely by Google) Google Privacy Policy

3. Permissions Requested

The Application requests only the minimal permissions necessary for core operation:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required for Google AdMob advertisement delivery and checking Google Play In-App Purchase status.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used by AdMob to verify network availability before requesting advertisements.

No storage permissions, camera permissions, or location permissions are required for the standard operation of the app.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Because all resume data is retained exclusively on your device, you have complete and instantaneous control over your data:

  • Deletion within App: You can delete any individual resume or clear resume fields directly inside the app interface.
  • Clear All Data: Clearing app storage in your Android device settings (Settings > Apps > Smart Resume > Clear Storage) permanently wipes all stored resumes.
  • Uninstalling: Uninstalling the application automatically deletes all locally saved resumes and application preferences.

5. Children's Privacy

These Services do not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. In the case we discover that a child under 13 has provided personal information, we immediately remove it from our records.

6. Security

We value your trust in creating your professional resume through our application. Because all resume files remain strictly in local device sandboxing, your sensitive career information is protected by Android's internal application sandboxing architecture. Please ensure your device is protected by a screen lock or biometric authentication.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page with an updated effective date.
