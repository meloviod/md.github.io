1. Overview

Welcome to Spend One Dollar (also localized in regional currencies on Google Play, package ID com.spendonedollar.spend). We respect your privacy and are committed to complete transparency. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you install and use our mobile application.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. Specifically, the application does not access or collect:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or physical address;
  • Device identifiers, IP addresses, or advertising IDs;
  • Precise or approximate geographic location;
  • Contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or local files;
  • Usage analytics, telemetry, or behavioral tracking profiles.

3. Local Device Storage

Any in-app preferences, counters, or interactive pastime progress are stored strictly locally on your device using standard Android application preferences (SharedPreferences). This data never leaves your device and is automatically erased if you uninstall the application or clear its storage in Android settings.

4. In-App Purchases & Google Play Billing

The application offers optional in-app purchases ("Spend More") processed exclusively through the official Google Play Billing Library.

  • All financial transactions and payment methods are handled directly and securely by Google Play.
  • We never receive, view, process, or store your credit card numbers, bank details, or billing address.
  • Google's processing of purchase transactions is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Permissions

The application only requests the standard com.android.vending.BILLING permission required by Google Play to process in-app purchases. No dangerous or sensitive runtime permissions are requested.

6. Children's Privacy

Because we do not collect any personal information from any user of any age, our application complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and international equivalents.

7. Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and similar privacy laws, users have rights to access, export, or delete their personal data. Because we do not collect or retain any personal data on external servers, there is no personal record held by us to disclose, sell, or delete.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect technical or regulatory changes. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or the application, please contact the developer via the support email listed on the official Google Play Store listing page for Spend One Dollar.

Мы не собираем, не храним, не передаём на сторонние серверы и не продаём никакие персональные данные. В частности, приложение не запрашивает и не собирает:

3. Локальное хранение на устройстве

Все внутренние настройки, счётчики и прогресс встроенных мини-режимов сохраняются исключительно локально на вашем устройстве с помощью стандартного механизма Android (SharedPreferences). Эти данные никогда не покидают ваше устройство и полностью удаляются при удалении приложения или очистке данных приложения в настройках Android.
