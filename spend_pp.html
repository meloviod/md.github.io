1. Overview

Welcome to Spend One Dollar (package ID com.spendonemoney). We respect your privacy and are committed to complete transparency. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you install and use our mobile application.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. Specifically, the application does not access or collect:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or physical address;
  • Precise or approximate geographic location;
  • Contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or private files;
  • User accounts or behavioral profiling.

3. Rewarded Advertising (Google AdMob)

The application contains an optional "Watch" feature that allows users to voluntarily view rewarded video advertisements in exchange for virtual in-game stars. These advertisements are provided by Google Mobile Ads (AdMob).

  • AdMob may process device identifiers and ad-related telemetry strictly to deliver and verify rewarded video ads, prevent fraud, and comply with Google's policies.
  • You can review Google's advertising policies and privacy terms at Google Ads & Privacy.

4. In-App Purchases & Google Play Billing

The application offers optional in-app purchases ("Spend More") processed exclusively through the official Google Play Billing Library.

  • All financial transactions and payment methods are handled directly and securely by Google Play.
  • We never receive, view, process, or store your credit card numbers, bank details, or billing address.
  • Google's processing of purchase transactions is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Local Device Storage

All counters, stars, and preferences are stored strictly locally on your device via standard Android SharedPreferences. This data never leaves your device and is automatically erased if you uninstall the application.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA / GDPR)

Because we do not collect any personal information from any user of any age, our application complies with COPPA and international equivalents.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or the application, please contact the developer via the support email listed on the official Google Play Store listing page for Spend One Dollar.
